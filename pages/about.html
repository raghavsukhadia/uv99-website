Why UV99 exists

We believe protection should never cloud the view.

UV99 turns an invisible science into a clear promise: remove the harmful part of sunlight while keeping the drive natural, sharp and connected.

“Harmful rays out. Protection in. Everything you love about the view stays exactly where it belongs.”

What guides us

Four principles, one honest product story.

01 · Clarity

Preserve the road as it is.

Protection is not permission for haze, colour shift or optical distortion. A film lives in the driver’s line of sight.

02 · Evidence

Use numbers people can compare.

Visible light, UV, infrared and total heat are different measures. We label them plainly and avoid pretending one is another.

03 · Restraint

Technology should disappear.

The best result feels factory-fitted: clean edges, neutral colour and no unnecessary visual noise.

04 · Accountability

Stand behind the installation.

Care guidance, traceable fitment records and written warranty terms turn a promise into responsibility.
