Privacy Policy

How UV99 collects, uses, shares and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated 1 September 2026 · UV99 Glazing

At UV99, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quotation, or use our products and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • City or service location
  • Vehicle or installation details
  • Product preferences
  • Information provided through enquiry or contact forms
  • Website usage and technical information such as browser, device, and IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests
  • Provide product and installation information
  • Schedule or manage installation services
  • Process warranty or service requests
  • Improve our products, services, and website
  • Send service-related communications
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry or request

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers or business partners only when reasonably necessary to provide our products or services, process enquiries, complete installations, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage, and enhance your browsing experience.

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. UV99 is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

UV99 may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact UV99 through the contact details provided on our website.

Reach us any time via the Contact page. See also our Terms & Conditions and Disclaimer.
