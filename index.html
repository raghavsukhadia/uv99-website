About UV99

An advanced glazing brand built on a single promise.

Uncompromising protection against harmful ultraviolet rays. Developed with precision UV-defense technology, every UV99 glazing product blocks up to 99% of harmful UV-A and UV-B rays — protecting your skin, your passengers, and your vehicle’s interior on every journey.

  • Interior Protection

    Shields the dashboard, seats, and other interior components from sun damage such as fading, cracking, and deterioration — keeping your cabin looking new for longer.

  • Skin Protection

    Protects your skin from damage caused by harmful UV rays while you drive.

  • Enhanced Comfort

    Creates a cooler, calmer environment inside the cabin, making every trip feel more pleasant and smooth.

  • Glare Reduction

    Cuts intense sunlight and reflections, making driving in bright conditions safer and more comfortable.

  • Optical Clarity

    Delivers clear, sharp visibility without distortion — day or night — so there is no compromise on what you see.
